Core strategic rule

Accumulation and continuity matter more than one isolated act.

The strategy distinguishes geographic, environmental, cartographic and institutional progress from territorial recognition. Each institution should be approached within its actual mandate.

Mission

Establish a durable international record of the geographical existence, environmental relevance, historical development and institutional significance of Gornja Siga through independent research, geospatial documentation, scientific publication, NGO participation and engagement with relevant European and international frameworks.

Central objective

Progressively strengthen the geographic and institutional position of Gornja Siga by creating a broad, cumulative network of evidence and engagement, while recognizing that no single organization can determine sovereignty or territorial status.
